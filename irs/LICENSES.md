# Impulse response licences

Every IR file committed under `irs/` must have a row here **before** it is committed. Only IRs whose
licence allows redistribution in this app may be bundled. No Neural DSP IRs.

The factory IRs below are not recordings of any product. They are generated by our own speaker-and-mic
model in `dsp/tools/irgen` (Krzysztof chose generated IRs on 2026-11-23), so they are original work of this
repository under its licence (GPL-3.0). Regenerate them with
`./build/release/tools/irgen/gigrig_irgen ../irs` from `dsp/`; the test "cab factory IRs" checks that the
committed files match the model.

All are mono, 48 kHz, 32-bit float, 4096 samples. "cap" is the brighter mic position at the cone centre,
"edge" the darker one at the cone edge.
